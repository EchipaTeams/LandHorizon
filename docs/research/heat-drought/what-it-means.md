## Relevance to LandHorizon
- **Terminology:** a "heatwave" or "drought" count needs its own stated threshold and reference period; WMO and EEA define them locally and statistically (claims 1, 2, 5). ANM numeric criteria could not be confirmed, so do not quote them (claim 6). "Drought" is three different things (claim 4); the label must say which.
- **Hazard, not risk:** IPCC risk needs hazard, exposure and vulnerability (claim 7). LandHorizon has none of exposure or vulnerability for a plot (crop type, irrigation, building), so the honest name is "hazard indicator from past observations" or "regional climate context".
- **Forecasts** are irrelevant to a purchase measured in decades (claim 8). **Projections** are regional (about 12 km at best, claim 9; ClimateCheck uses about 5 km, claim 12) and must be worded as scenario-conditional; vendors do show them, but the sources here give no wording rules (not found).
- **Scale:** projection and gridded observation products are far coarser than 1 to 5 ha plots, so a per-plot number would largely repeat one regional value (claims 9, 12). Independent critique supports caution: models agree regionally and diverge at property level (claims 13, 14). Plot-scale variation of surface temperature exists but was not sourced beyond leads.
- **Pilot hazard is real:** aridity trend and rising drought frequency in the south-east (claims 15, 16, 17).
- **Honest wording options and what must hold:**
  1. "Regional climate context, same for every plot in the area": defensible with any gridded past-observation statistic if the report names threshold, period and grid size and says it is not plot-specific.
  2. "Hazard indicator for the plot's surroundings" (past observations, state the cell size): defensible if cell size is stated and missing data is `unknown`.
  3. "Plot-level risk": not supported; would need exposure and vulnerability inputs the product lacks, a validated plot-scale source and independent validation; the vendor-critique evidence cuts against claiming it.
  4. Projection statements: only as "under scenario X, region Y shows change Z", never a prediction, and not as a score.
- Data access, licence, cost, compute, coverage: not found in these sources (out of scope). Under the project constraints a new hazard after the 22 Nov gate is a roadmap item; wording option 1 is the cheapest honest pitch line.
